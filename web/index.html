Methods and limitations

Balanced, complete ratings only. The same set of k raters must score every subject for two-way ICCs. ICC(3) here is the consistency formulation; mixed-model absolute agreement requires a different interpretation. Negative estimates are not silently truncated. Undefined estimates are labeled “Not estimable”.

This version reports point estimates, not confidence intervals or hypothesis tests. Do not interpret thresholds as clinical decisions without domain-specific validation.

Method: Shrout PE, Fleiss JL (1979); Koo TK, Li MY (2016), guideline for selecting ICCs. Source repository.

Privacy: entered ratings are processed by local JavaScript and are not transmitted or stored by this page. The page uses no third-party scripts or fonts. Avoid entering patient-identifiable data.
